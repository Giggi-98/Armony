# Armony bot — Terms of Service

*Italiano più sotto.*

Armony is free, open source software (https://github.com/Giggi-98/Armony). The Armony bot is run by the owner of a private Armony music server for themselves and their friends.

- **Who can use it.** Only people who have an account on the owner's Armony server can link their Discord account and use the commands that act on the server. Everyone else only sees the bot's replies in channels where it was added.
- **What it does.** It searches the server's music library, sends notifications chosen by the user, plays music from the library in voice channels, and posts activity in channels that the server administrator connected.
- **Acceptable use.** Do not use the bot to share content you have no right to share, to spam, or to break Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The administrator can unlink any account at any time.
- **No warranty.** The bot is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, and may stop working at any time.
- **Changes.** These terms may change; the current version is always at this address.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/Giggi-98/Armony/issues
